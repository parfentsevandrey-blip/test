The Residence

An address that exists only in the sky.

Occupying an entire wing of one of Moscow-City's most distinguished towers, ZENITH is a residence conceived for those who measure luxury not in square metres, but in altitude, light and silence.

Floor-to-ceiling glass wraps the home in the Moskva River, the Kremlin, and an endless horizon. Interiors are delivered turnkey by a Milanese atelier — Italian stone, hand-laid oak, and bronze detailing throughout.

  • 01 Private high-speed elevator opening into your own lobby
  • 02 Four bedroom suites, each with river-facing dressing rooms
  • 03 Boffi kitchen, Gaggenau appliances, dedicated wine room
  • 04 Whole-home climate, lighting & shading automation
